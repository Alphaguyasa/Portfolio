- **Long:** Small shops in Ethiopia take payment through mobile money and bank apps, and the confirmations arrive as notifications nobody reconciles against who owes the shop. #### Suq: Offline-first point of sale and inventory

- **Short:** Offline-first point of sale and inventory. IndexedDB is the source of truth and Supabase is a replica.
- **Long:** A shop till has to keep selling when the internet and the power go out, and two offline tablets must not overwrite each other's sales when they reconnect. IndexedDB is the source of truth and Supabase is a replica. Stock is never synced as a quantity: each device appends stock movements, and stock on hand is their sum, so concurrent offline sales commute. Sales are append-only; a mistake becomes a void row, not a deletion. A sale writes to IndexedDB in one transaction and never touches the network. A sync test suite covers the five failure modes that only appear across multiple devices, including two tablets selling the last unit while offline. Amharic-first UI, ETB pricing and 80mm thermal receipts.
- **Status:** In development · last commit 2026-11-03. Under active development. CI passes on every merge; no public deployment is linked from the repo.
- **Skills:** Next.js, TypeScript, Dexie (IndexedDB), Supabase, Serwist
- **Link:** https://github.com/Alphaguyasa/Suq

#### DocSearch → Not Alone: Cited answers from documents, now pivoting to a scripture app

- **Short:** Cited answers from documents, now pivoting to a scripture app. Hybrid retrieval: vector search and Postgres full-text search fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion.
- **Long:** Question answering over documents is only useful if every claim can be traced to a source, and if the system refuses when the documents do not cover the question. Hybrid retrieval: vector search and Postgres full-text search fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion. Every claim in the streamed answer carries an inline citation to its source chunk. An evaluation harness measures retrieval and answer quality against a human-reviewed golden set. The pivot, Not Alone, answers a described struggle with the cited story of a holy person who fell the same way and was restored. 91 human-reviewed evaluation questions. One of four LLM judges validated against human labels: Cohen's kappa 0.80 on a random sample. Answer correctness 52.4% on the dev split; 24 of 63 answerable questions get a refusal.
- **Status:** In development · last commit 2026-11-03. Pivoting. The cited-retrieval engine is being repurposed into Not Alone, a scripture app; the original DocSearch evaluation work is complete.
- **Skills:** Next.js, TypeScript, Postgres + pgvector, Voyage embeddings, Anthropic / Gemini
- **Link:** https://github.com/Alphaguyasa/docsearch

#### ethiopic-text: Search and normalization for Ethiopic script

- **Short:** Search and normalization for Ethiopic script. Fold the homophone families with codepoint arithmetic rather than a lookup table, in independently importable modules for folding, search, fuzzy matching, transliteration, Geez numerals, phone numbers and money..
- **Long:** Ethiopic has homophone consonants that writers use interchangeably, so the same word is spelled two ways and string search silently misses one of them. Fold the homophone families with codepoint arithmetic rather than a lookup table, in independently importable modules for folding, search, fuzzy matching, transliteration, Geez numerals, phone numbers and money. Zero runtime dependencies; the core is 0.49 KB gzipped. 100% coverage.

- **Skills:** TypeScript
